What is a focus block?
A stretch of time for one task only. Notifications wait, the timer runs, and the space stays quiet until it ends.
Can I share a space?
Yes. Invite up to five people; everyone sees the same plan, and only you see your notes.
What happens to archived spaces?
They leave your sidebar but keep everything. Restore one at any time from Settings.
