Accordion

Questions that open in place. An open item rises a level and its knob turns mint.

Accordion

When is an invoice overdue?
The day after its due date. We send the client a friendly nudge three days before, and again on the day.
Can clients pay by bank transfer?
Yes. Every invoice shows your bank details next to the card button, and transfers are matched automatically.
How do refunds work?
Refunds go back to the original card within five working days. The invoice is marked “Refunded”.
